## Overview

### From team merch to a driver's own brand

Mark Ivanov is a Slovenian formula driver, and I've been working with him for about three years.

The project started as a merch store for Walter Wolf Racing, the former Formula 1 team. Circumstances changed, and it became something bigger: Mark's own brand, **MIA (Mark Ivanov Automotive)**, which makes the first fully flush aerodiscs, made for the Porsche 992 GT3 RS.

I designed and developed the store on **Shopify Hydrogen**, Shopify's React framework for custom storefronts. The site is still private.

![The landing page hero: a Porsche 992 GT3 RS on track with the headline Born from racing. Flush by design.](/media/mark-ivanov/hero.webp "The landing page opens on the car, on track, with the product on its wheels.")

## The story

### Selling a product nobody has seen before

An aerodisc is a cover that closes the wheel for better airflow. MIA's sits level with the rim, and almost nobody has seen one before. So the landing page isn't a catalog. It tells the story first: who made the discs, why, and where they were tested.

The manifesto lights up word by word as you scroll, between a marquee of the brand's lines and the first close-up of the car.

![A black marquee with Fully flush, Porsche 992 GT3 RS and Born from racing, above the manifesto Seven years of hill climbs taught us that small details decide. So we closed the wheel.](/media/mark-ivanov/manifesto.webp "The manifesto, lit word by word as you scroll.")

### Three reasons, one pinned section

"Nothing sticks out" explains the product in three steps: flush, built for airflow, and raced first. The section stays pinned while you scroll, and each step brings its own photo.

![The Why flush section with step two, Built for airflow, active next to a photo of the camo prototype disc on the wheel](/media/mark-ivanov/why-flush.webp "Each step swaps the photo as you scroll through it.")

### Racing credentials, kept short

Mark's racing record is what makes the product credible, so it gets its own space: four numbers, then a timeline from hill climbs to the 992. It covers seven years of hill climbs, the 2024 Junior National and Slovenian Cup titles, and the first prototype discs, raced on a Lancer Evo IX.

![Four stats, 1st fully flush aerodisc, 7 years of hill climbs, 3 titles and records in 2024 and 992 GT3 RS fitment, above the timeline From the hill to the 992](/media/mark-ivanov/stats.webp "The numbers first, then the timeline.")

![Mark Ivanov sitting against the wheel of the GT3 RS next to the quote Built from racing. Driven by engineering.](/media/mark-ivanov/founder.webp "The founder section, in Mark's own words.")

### On track, and one set for sale

A gallery that scrolls sideways shows the car on track, from night sessions to details of the livery. It links to the brand's Instagram, where most of the audience already is.

![The On track gallery: a row of photos of the blue GT3 RS with captions and numbers](/media/mark-ivanov/on-track.webp "A sideways gallery of the car on track.")

The prototype set from the first track day is for sale too. It's a one-off, so instead of a checkout it gets a short list and a button to message Mark directly.

![Mark crouching next to the GT3 RS with the camo prototype discs, next to The prototype set is for sale](/media/mark-ivanov/prototype-sale.webp "The one-off prototype set, sold by message.")

## The configurator

### Pick a finish, see it from every angle

The product page is a configurator. You choose one of five finishes, from carbon twill to the prototype camo and signal red, and drag the disc to see it from every angle. A Photos tab switches to real photos of the discs on the car.

![The configurator with the prototype camo disc rotated in the 360° viewer, next to the MIA Aerodiscs buy box](/media/mark-ivanov/configurator-camo.webp "The 360° viewer with the prototype camo finish.")

![The configurator's Photos tab, with a photo of the camo disc on the wheel and four thumbnails below](/media/mark-ivanov/configurator-photos.webp "The Photos tab shows the discs on the car.")

### Built for a sold-out launch

The current run is sold out, so the store is designed for that. There's no price and no "add to cart". The main button is "Notify me", which sends you to Instagram to get in line for the next set. Under it, delivery, the right of withdrawal and the guarantee are stated plainly, because the store only sells within the EU.

Further down are the technical data and the questions buyers ask before ordering, like whether the discs affect brake cooling or are road legal. A bar stays at the bottom of the screen with the chosen finish and the main action.

![The technical data table with fitment and construction, and the sticky bar with the selected finish and Notify me](/media/mark-ivanov/specs.webp "Technical data, with the selected finish always in the sticky bar.")

## Every screen

### Mobile first, down to the 404

Most of the audience comes from Instagram, on a phone. Every section was designed for mobile too, from the hero to the buy box.

![Four mobile screens: the hero, the founder section, the configurator and the buy box](/media/mark-ivanov/mobile.webp "The hero, founder, configurator and buy box on a phone.")

The same voice runs through the whole store. The footer ends on the logo at full width, and the 404 page says you went "off the racing line".
